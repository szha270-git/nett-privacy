Support

Need help with Nett?

Find answers to common questions and share non-sensitive feedback.

Get in touch

For general help, feature suggestions and bug reports, open a support issue on GitHub. Please provide your iOS version, Nett version and a description of the problem.

Important: GitHub issues are public. Do not post bank statements, financial details, account numbers, recovery credentials or other personal information.

Which statements can I import?

Nett currently focuses on Australian American Express PDF statements and NAB CSV transaction files. Other formats may not work. Always review dates, amounts and merchants before confirming an import.

Does Nett connect to my bank or Apple Pay?

No direct bank-account sync or automatic Apple Pay transaction capture is included in the initial release. Supported transactions are imported from files you select.

What if a transaction cannot be read?

Review unreadable source details, ambiguous dates or transactions outside the statement period. Do not force incorrect amounts or dates just to complete an import.

Can I change categories?

Yes. You can edit transactions and categories, with optional on-device categorisation assistance on supported devices.

What if Apple Intelligence is unavailable?

Core import and review features remain available. You can categorise transactions manually.

How do I keep a backup?

Use Nett's encrypted backup export and store the resulting file and recovery credentials safely. Restoring may replace current ledger data, so read the preview carefully.

What are Keepsakes?

Keepsakes are editable mementos based on patterns in your recorded transactions. They are not independent proof of trips, events or other circumstances.
