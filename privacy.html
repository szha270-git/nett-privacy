Privacy Policy

Privacy comes first.

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Nett is a personal spending journal for iPhone. This policy describes how Nett is designed to handle information you enter or import. It covers the Nett app, rather than independent third-party services you choose to use.

Information you use in Nett

You may import financial statements and store transaction descriptions, amounts, dates, merchant names, account labels, merchant aliases, category choices, notes and Keepsakes. Nett uses these records to provide import review, personal spending analysis, categorisation and collection features.

Local storage and processing

Nett is designed to store your ledger and Keepsakes locally on your device. Supported statement files are parsed on your device, and optional Apple Intelligence-assisted categorisation is designed to use on-device processing where available. Nett does not require a Nett account, direct bank connection or bank synchronisation for its supported file-import features.

Nett is not designed to upload your imported statements, ledger or Keepsakes to a server operated by the developer. Your use of Apple's device, operating-system and App Store services is subject to Apple's own privacy practices.

Exported files and encrypted backups

When you choose to export an encrypted Nett backup or another file, you decide where to save or share it. This may include services such as iCloud Drive or another file provider, which handle the file under their own privacy policies. Keep backup files and any associated recovery credentials secure. Deleting Nett from your device does not delete copies you exported elsewhere.

Restoring a backup may replace the information currently stored in Nett. Please review the app's restore preview and confirmation before proceeding.

Data sharing and third-party services

Nett is not designed to transmit your financial records to the developer for advertising, profiling or sale. Nett does not connect directly to your bank. If you choose to share files, store backups using an external provider, or contact support through GitHub, those services process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Your choices and data deletion

You can review and edit information through the controls available in Nett. You can export backups for your own safekeeping and remove the app from your device. Removing the app normally removes data in its local app container, but does not remove separately exported files or copies that may remain in device backups or third-party services.

Children

Nett is a personal finance recordkeeping tool and is not specifically directed to children.

Policy changes

We may update this policy as Nett's features or data handling change. The date at the top of this page identifies the most recent revision.

Contact

For general privacy questions, you can open a GitHub issue. GitHub issues are public: never include bank statements, account numbers, passwords or other private information in a public issue. For general assistance, see Nett Support.
